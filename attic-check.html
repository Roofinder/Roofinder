Check it yourself first

The joist test takes two minutes.

Pop your head up through the attic hatch and look at the wooden beams running across the floor.

If you can see the tops of the beams, the insulation is level with them or lower, and there is probably not enough.

If the beams are buried and you can't see them, you may already be fine. The check will confirm it either way, and there's no charge to hear "you're good".

What gets checked

Insulation is only half of it.

More insulation over leaky gaps or a stuffy attic doesn't do much. A proper check covers all four.

  1. 1

    Depth and coverage

    How many inches you have, what it adds up to, and any bare or packed-down patches.

  2. 2

    Air leaks

    Gaps around light fixtures, pipes, wiring, the attic hatch and wall tops let conditioned air escape. These get sealed before anything is added.

  3. 3

    Ventilation

    Air should come in low at the eaves and leave high at the ridge. If it can't, the attic cooks in summer and holds moisture in winter.

  4. 4

    Moisture

    Bath fans venting into the attic, damp wood, dark stains or mold.

The roof connection

A hot attic is hard on the roof, too.

When an attic can't breathe, summer heat builds up under the shingles and they age faster. Winter moisture can rot the decking from the inside.

That's why we ask if you want the roof looked at on the same visit. One trip up the ladder, one set of photos. Roof check only?

Questions

Before you book

Is the check really free?

Yes. If it needs work, you get a written quote. You can say no and owe nothing.

Who actually comes out?

A local insulation or roofing company we work with. They'll give you their company name when they call, before anyone comes to the house.

Am I going to get a pile of calls?

No. Your details go to one company, not a list of contractors bidding for the job.

Is there a tax credit?

The federal tax credit for insulation ended for work done after 2025. Duke Energy has run insulation rebates in North Carolina before. Ask the company what's available now, and don't let anyone promise you a rebate they can't show you in writing.

How long does the work take?

For most homes, adding blown-in insulation is a single day. Air sealing and ventilation work can add time. The quote will say.

Do I need to be home?

Yes for the check. They need to get into the attic and walk you through the photos.

Who is RooFinder?

We connect homeowners with local roofing and insulation companies. We don't do the work ourselves, and the company pays us a referral fee if you hire them. You deal with them directly and pay them, not us.
