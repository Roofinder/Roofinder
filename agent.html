📋 Door Script — Cary Pocket 82
STEP 1 — CONFIRM NAME
Knock and step back. When they answer:

"Hey, is this [Name from your list]?"
STEP 2 — THE OPENER
"Hey [Name], I'm [Your Name] — I work with a roofing contractor in your area. I was driving through the neighborhood and noticed a lot of the roofs out here are getting some age on them — yours included. Didn't want to just skip past without stopping."

"Has anyone taken a look at your roof recently?"
STEP 3 — BUILD INTEREST
"We've been helping homeowners in this zip get a free inspection — we check the age, condition, and whether there's any storm damage that your insurance might actually cover. A lot of people are surprised what they qualify for."

"It's completely free, no obligation. We're just looking to be a resource for the neighborhood."
STEP 4 — CLOSE FOR INSPECTION
"We could get someone out here in the next few days — would you be open to a free 15-minute roof inspection? You'd get a full condition report, and if there's nothing urgent, you'd at least know where you stand."

[If yes → get their number and lock in a date/time]
💬 Objections
"We just had it done / replaced it."
"That's great — that's exactly the kind of thing we try to help people stay ahead of. If it's been done recently, you're in good shape. If you ever need documentation for insurance or want a second opinion down the road, we're always around. Mind if I leave a card?"
"Not interested" / "We're good."
"Totally understand. It's free and quick, but I get it — no worries at all. If you ever notice anything or want someone to take a look, feel free to reach out." [Leave card, move on.]
"How old is my roof?"
"Based on when homes in this area were built, yours is around [roof age from your list] years old. That's right in the window where we like to take a closer look — most manufacturers warranty shingles for 20–25 years."
"Is this going to cost anything?"
"Nothing at all. The inspection is free, no strings attached. If we find damage, we'll walk you through your options — including whether insurance covers it. If everything looks fine, we'll tell you that too."
"I need to talk to my spouse first."
"Of course — makes total sense. Can I leave a card and check back in a couple days? Or if it's easier, I can send you a quick text so you have my info ready when you chat."
"How did you get my address / who gave you my info?"
"We work this neighborhood as part of our territory — we go street by street. It's all public information, nothing unusual. We just prefer to knock and introduce ourselves rather than cold call."
