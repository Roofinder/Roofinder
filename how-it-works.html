What "Lead" Actually Means Here

These are targeted doors, not inbound inquiries. A RooFinder "lead" is a homeowner who fits the profile — aging roof, right home value, owner-occupied — and lives in your exclusive zone.

Your agents knock those doors. Some answer, some don't. Some are interested, some aren't. The platform gives you the best possible doors and the tools to work them effectively. Your team still has to knock and sell.

08 — Who This Is Not For

We'd Rather Say It Upfront
Than Waste Your Time.

RooFinder works extremely well for the right company. But it's not the right fit for everyone — and we'd rather tell you now than have you find out after month one.

✗
Companies expecting booked appointments without knocking
This is a canvassing system. Your agents knock doors. If you're looking for inbound leads or pre-booked appointments delivered to you, this isn't what we do.
✗
Operations with no door-to-door reps
RooFinder is built around a field team. If you don't have agents who will hit the streets, the zone won't produce results — no matter how good the addresses are.
✗
Teams looking for a guaranteed close rate
We guarantee the best possible doors and a platform that makes your team more effective. We don't guarantee how many close — that depends on your reps and your pitch.
✗
Companies not willing to commit to consistent canvassing
Canvassing compounds over time. If your team knocks two days then stops for two weeks, the system won't perform. This works for operators who show up consistently.
This Is For You If…
✓You have reps who will go door-to-door — or you're ready to build that team
✓You want to stop depending on company-provided leads and build your own pipeline
✓You want to know exactly where your team is, what they've knocked, and what's in the pipeline — without making calls
✓You're serious about running a structured, repeatable field sales operation — not a random door-knocking effort
